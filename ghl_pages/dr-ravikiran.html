Professional Biography & Clinical Experience

Dr. Ravikiran N. Tamragouri, MD, FACP, is an esteemed board-certified internal medicine physician and a distinguished Fellow of the American College of Physicians (FACP) with decades of dedicated service in patient care. He earned his medical degree from the prestigious Bangalore Medical College and completed his internal medicine residency training at Grant Hospital in Chicago, establishing an exceptional foundation in evidence-based diagnostics and chronic disease management.

With a substantial clinical focus on general adult health and geriatrics, Dr. Tamragouri specializes in managing complex, overlapping chronic diseases such as Type 2 diabetes, heart disease, dementia, and cellulitis. His clinical colleagues and patients recognize him for his remarkable clinical thoroughness, thoughtful diagnostic investigations, and genuine warmth.

Dr. Tamragouri maintains active hospital admitting privileges with premier medical institutions in the western suburbs, including Advocate Good Samaritan Hospital in Downers Grove and UChicago Medicine AdventHealth Hinsdale. At Fairview Ogden Medical Group, he is dedicated to forming lifelong relationships with patients and families based on clear communication and clinical excellence.
