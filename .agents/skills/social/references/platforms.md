## LinkedIn

**Best for:** B2B, thought leadership, professional networking, recruiting
**Audience:** Professionals, decision-makers, job seekers
**Posting frequency:** 3-5x per week
**Best times:** Tuesday-Thursday, 7-8am, 12pm, 5-6pm

**What works:**
- Personal stories with business lessons
- Contrarian takes on industry topics
- Behind-the-scenes of building a company
- Data and original insights
- Carousel posts (document format)
- Polls that spark discussion

**What doesn't:**
- Overly promotional content
- Generic motivational quotes
- Links in the main post (kills reach)
- Corporate speak without personality

**Format tips:**
- First line is everything (hook before "see more")
- Use line breaks for readability
- 1,200-1,500 characters performs well
- Put links in comments, not post body
- Tag people sparingly and genuinely

**Algorithm tips:**
- First hour engagement matters most
- Comments > reactions > clicks
- Dwell time (people reading) signals quality
- No external links in post body
- Document posts (carousels) get strong reach
- Polls drive engagement but don't build authority

## Twitter/X

**Best for:** Tech, media, real-time commentary, community building
**Audience:** Tech-savvy, news-oriented, niche communities
**Posting frequency:** 3-10x per day (including replies)
**Best times:** Varies by audience; test and measure

**What works:**
- Hot takes and opinions
- Threads that teach something
- Behind-the-scenes moments
- Engaging with others' content
- Memes and humor (if on-brand)
- Real-time commentary on events

**What doesn't:**
- Pure self-promotion
- Threads without a strong hook
- Ignoring replies and mentions
- Scheduling everything (no real-time presence)

**Format tips:**
- Tweets under 100 characters get more engagement
- Threads: Hook in tweet 1, promise value, deliver
- Quote tweets with added insight beat plain retweets
- Use visuals to stop the scroll

**Algorithm tips:**
- Replies and quote tweets build authority
- Threads keep people on platform (rewarded)
- Images and video get more reach
- Engagement in first 30 min matters
- Twitter Blue/Premium may boost reach

## Threads (Meta)

**Best for:** Conversational takes, discussions, bridging Instagram audience, casual community
**Audience:** Broad, mainstream, creator- and lifestyle-adjacent, mobile-first
**Posting frequency:** 1-2x per day
**Best times:** 10am-12pm, 7-9pm

**What works:**
- Thought-provoking, casual questions that invite replies
- Honest reflections, behind-the-scenes insights, and relatable lessons
- Bite-sized contrarian takes with a conversational tone
- Engaging actively in your own comment threads
- Single topic tags (not hashtag walls) to categorize the post

**What doesn't:**
- Heavy corporate speak or overly formal B2B tone
- Hashtag-stuffing (Threads only supports 1 topic tag per post)
- Pure broadcast announcements without prompt for dialogue
- Cross-posting raw LinkedIn posts without tone adjustment

**Format tips:**
- Keep posts punchy (up to 500 characters standard)
- Use exactly 1 topic tag per post
- Break thoughts into short, readable 1-2 sentence paragraphs
- When repurposing long-form content, post the core controversy or question as the main post, then expand in replies

**Algorithm tips:**
- Meta's algorithm heavily rewards reply volume and conversation depth
- Posts get cross-promoted directly into Instagram feeds, exposing you to non-followers
- Native discussion outweighs external link clicks

## Bluesky

**Best for:** Tech, developer community, decentralization advocates, real-time commentary, open web
**Audience:** Tech-savvy, journalists, academics, open-source builders
**Posting frequency:** 3-10x per day (including replies and text threads)
**Best times:** Weekdays, morning (8-10am) and early evening (4-7pm)

**Guideline alignment:**
*Follows the same core playbook and voice as Twitter/X (hot takes, tactical threads, engaging in replies, concise hook-driven text), but is strictly optimized here for **text-only posts without images**.*

**What works:**
- Hot takes, concise lessons, and sharp observations
- Text-only threads breaking down complex ideas step-by-step
- Sharing direct links with clean native card previews (no algorithmic penalty for links!)
- Direct community engagement, quoting, and reply conversations
- Custom feeds and domain-verified handles (e.g. `@brand.com`) for credibility

**What doesn't:**
- Images (per workspace guideline: keep Bluesky focused purely on high-signal text copy)
- Engagement-bait ("Like this if you agree") or hollow growth-hacking tactics
- Corporate jargon or overly sterile PR voice
- Hashtag spam (keep to 1-2 or rely on custom topic feeds)

**Format tips:**
- Strict 300-character limit per post (plan thread breaks accordingly)
- Hook in post 1 with a standalone compelling statement
- Number thread posts (1/n, 2/n) for readability
- Deliver direct value without filler words

**Algorithm tips:**
- Bluesky relies on chronological feeds + customizable community algorithms (Feeds)
- Domain handles build instant authority and trust
- Links do not receive algorithmic downranking like they do on X or LinkedIn

## Google Business Profile (GBP)

**Best for:** Local SEO, search visibility on Google Maps and Google Search Knowledge Panels, brand credibility, capturing high-intent searchers
**Audience:** High-intent prospects, local clients, decision-makers researching Ruya directly on Google
**Posting frequency:** 1-2x per week
**Best times:** Weekday mornings (8-10am local time)

**What works:**
- "What's New" posts highlighting new product features, blog posts, or case studies
- "Event" posts for upcoming webinars, live demos, or conferences
- "Offer" posts for pilot programs, free consultations, or trials
- Clear, customer-focused language emphasizing solutions
- Highlighting real-world benefits and hospital operational outcomes

**What doesn't:**
- Generic social banter, memes, or informal chatter
- Putting phone numbers or pricing in post copy (Google spam filters often reject posts with phone numbers; use the official "Call now" button instead)
- Hashtags (Google Business Profile does not use hashtags; they clutter the post and signal low quality)
- Vague headlines that don't make sense before the mobile cutoff

**Format tips:**
- **The 80–100 Character Rule:** Only the first 80–100 characters show before the "More" truncation on Google Maps and mobile search. Your hook and core point MUST be in those first 80 characters.
- Keep total length between 150–300 characters (max limit is 1,500, but shorter performs best).
- Always include an explicit action button: "Learn more", "Sign up", "Book", or "Call now".
- Always append UTM parameters to the button URL (e.g., `?utm_source=google_business_profile&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=post_title`) to track search conversions in GA4.

**Algorithm & Local SEO tips:**
- Active weekly posting sends positive freshness and recency signals to Google's local ranking algorithm
- Posts remain live for 6 months before archiving, but recency in the carousel drives top visibility
- Keywords in posts help Google match search queries for services and solutions

## Instagram

**Best for:** Visual brands, educational frameworks, B2B thought leadership via carousels
**Audience:** Visual-first professionals, healthcare facility peers
**Posting frequency:** 1 carousel post per scheduled blog cycle (1-2x/week)
**Best times:** 11am-1pm, 5-7pm

**Workspace Production Rule:**
*Instagram content in this workspace is **strictly 5-slide carousels only (No Reels)**. Each carousel is created by authoring a 5-card HTML/CSS layout and capturing high-resolution JPEG screenshots (1080×1350, 4:5 ratio).*

**What works:**
- Clean, high-contrast 5-slide educational decks
- Slide 1 designed as a standalone scroll-stopper hook
- Clear visual hierarchy with bold typography
- Captions incorporating relevant target keywords from `Keywords/rank-tracking-ruyacompliance.com.csv`
- 3–5 targeted hashtags at the end of the caption

**What doesn't:**
- Reels or short-form video (excluded per workspace policy)
- Carousels longer than 5 slides
- Low-contrast or cluttered slide graphics
- Generic stock photos without educational value

**Format tips:**
- Exactly 5 slides per carousel
- 1080×1350 (4:5) aspect ratio
- Render slides via HTML/CSS code and capture as crisp JPEG images
- Slide 5 serves as the summary and single conversion CTA

## TikTok

**Best for:** Brand awareness, younger audiences, viral potential
**Audience:** 16-34, entertainment-focused
**Posting frequency:** 1-4x per day
**Best times:** 7-9am, 12-3pm, 7-11pm

**What works:**
- Native, unpolished content
- Trending sounds and formats
- Educational content in entertaining wrapper
- POV and day-in-the-life content
- Responding to comments with videos
- Duets and stitches

**What doesn't:**
- Overly produced content
- Ignoring trends
- Hard selling
- Repurposed horizontal video

**Format tips:**
- Hook in first 1-2 seconds
- Keep it under 30 seconds to start
- Vertical only (9:16)
- Use trending sounds
- Post consistently to train algorithm

## Facebook

**Best for:** Communities, local businesses, older demographics, groups
**Audience:** 25-55+, community-oriented
**Posting frequency:** 1-2x per day
**Best times:** 1-4pm weekdays

**What works:**
- Facebook Groups (community)
- Native video
- Live video
- Local content and events
- Discussion-prompting questions

**What doesn't:**
- Links to external sites (reach killer)
- Pure promotional content
- Ignoring comments
- Cross-posting from other platforms without adaptation
