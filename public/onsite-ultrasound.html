Available Onsite Diagnostic Ultrasounds

Echocardiogram (Cardiac Scan)

Evaluates cardiac chambers, valvular function, ejection fraction, and wall motion to diagnose murmurs and heart failure.

Prep: No preparation needed

Carotid Artery Doppler

High-precision vascular scan assessing blood velocity and identifying atherosclerotic arterial plaque to prevent stroke.

Prep: Loose-collared clothing

Abdominal Ultrasound

Detailed imaging of liver, gallbladder (gallstones), kidneys, and pancreas for abdominal pain and enzyme elevations.

Prep: Fasting 6 to 8 hours

Thyroid Sonography

High-resolution assessment of thyroid gland dimensions and nodule characterization under ACR TI-RADS guidelines.

Prep: No necklaces or high collars

Aortic Aneurysm (AAA) Screening

Non-invasive aortic diameter measurement detecting asymptomatic aneurysms in adults and smokers.

Prep: Fasting 6 hours

Pelvic Ultrasound

Evaluates bladder, pelvic floor, and reproductive anatomy to investigate hematuria, pelvic pain, or abnormalities.

Prep: Full bladder required

Frequently Answered Questions

Who provides the ultrasound services at Fairview Ogden Medical Group?

Our onsite ultrasound services are offered in direct clinical partnership with Precision Imaging (https://precisionimagingus.com). Registered diagnostic medical sonographers (ARDMS) utilize high-resolution sonographic equipment with fast interpretations from board-certified radiologists.

Is diagnostic ultrasound safe and radiation-free?

Yes. Ultrasound uses sound waves instead of ionizing radiation, making it 100% painless, safe, and free from radiation risks associated with X-rays or CT scans.

Are diagnostic ultrasounds covered by Medicare and commercial insurance?

Yes. Echocardiograms, Doppler exams, and abdominal ultrasounds with medical necessity are covered benefits under Medicare, Illinois Medicaid, and private commercial health plans.
