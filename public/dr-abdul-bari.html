Professional Biography & Clinical Leadership

Dr. Abdul-Bari Syed, DO, is a board-certified internal medicine physician and hospitalist with nearly two decades of clinical experience managing complex acute and chronic conditions in adult patients. He completed his medical degree at Des Moines University College of Osteopathic Medicine and went on to complete his internal medicine residency training at the University of Illinois College of Medicine at Chicago.

Before attending medical school, Dr. Syed earned a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from Benedictine University in Lisle, Illinois, giving him a unique clinical foundation in understanding the psychological and behavioral facets of patient wellness, chronic disease burden, and lifestyle transformation.

Since 2010, Dr. Syed has served extensively as an internal medicine hospitalist, directing acute inpatient medical units, post-acute rehabilitation facilities, and multidisciplinary clinical teams across regional hospital networks. At Fairview Ogden Medical Group, Dr. Syed serves as the Clinical Director of Sleep Lab Services, overseeing our state-of-the-art overnight polysomnography program and delivering hospital-grade diagnostic care directly to the Downers Grove outpatient setting.
